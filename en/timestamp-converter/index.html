Complete guide to using the Unix Timestamp conversion tool

Unix Timestamp represents the elapsed time since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC. Common units are seconds or milliseconds. The most confusing thing when converting is the unit and time zone: the timestamp itself represents the instant, and the formatted text will display different dates according to the time zone.

Operation steps

  1. Confirm whether the source is seconds, milliseconds or other precision
  2. Enter a timestamp or date and select the time zone you want to view
  3. Compare UTC to local results before using in database or API

Practical suggestions and limitations

Don't make arbitrary assumptions about date strings without time zone information. Cross-system exchange is recommended to use ISO 8601 with an explicit Z or offset, and be aware of the range limit of 32-bit seconds.

Privacy and native processing

Date and time stamps are converted locally in the browser and no itineraries or records are sent.
