Formula and variable relationship

Calculates balanced three-phase real power from line voltage, current, and PF. The implemented expression is “Math.sqrt(3)*voltage*current*pf/1000”. The page validates line voltage, line current, power factor before formatting the result in kW.

Sensitivity check: change only line voltage from 400 V to 440 V. With every other input unchanged, three-phase real power moves from 19.953 to 21.949 kW. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
