Complete guide to using the image watermark batch tool

Watermarks can show the source and reduce the convenience of unauthorized direct use. They are suitable for work previews, product images and delivery samples. It's not full DRM, and tags that are too small, too light, or just placed in the margins may still be cropped or repaired.

Operation steps

  1. Add an image and choose text or logo watermark
  2. Adjust position, repeat, scale, transparency and safety margins
  3. Preview the readability of each image one by one and then output them in batches, while retaining the original files without watermarks.

Practical suggestions and limitations

Watermarks should strike a balance between being legible and not disruptive to the subject. It is still recommended to reduce the public images to the actual required resolution and save the copyright evidence and original files.

Privacy and native processing

Images and logos are only synthesized in the local Canvas, and unpublished works will not be uploaded.
