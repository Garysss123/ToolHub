Frequently Asked Questions

SEO Content Analyzer (SEO Content Analyzer) is a free online tool that allows you to paste article or web content and automatically analyze the content quality, readability and SEO performance. The tool calculates indicators such as word count, keyword density, sentence structure, reading time, etc., and provides specific suggestions for improvement. All analysis is completed in the browser, no need to upload data, ensuring the privacy of your content.
It is generally recommended that the keyword density be maintained at 0.5%～2.5% between. Less than 0.5% indicates insufficient keyword usage, and search engines may not be able to effectively determine the topic of the page. If it is higher than 5%, it may be regarded as keyword stuffing (Keyword Stuffing), and there is a risk of being penalized by search engines. The ideal approach is to incorporate keywords naturally into your article, especially in the title, H1, first paragraph, and H2 subheadings in moderation. Remember, the relevance and quality of your content is far more important than keyword density.
Content length is one of the important factors for SEO ranking. According to multiple studies, the average content length of the top ten search results is 1500～2500 words between. Longer content typically covers the topic more comprehensively, provides more value to the reader, and is more likely to garner external links. However, this does not mean that you should fill it with meaningless content just to increase the word count. The focus is on providing complete and valuable information, and quality is always more important than quantity. It is generally recommended that the article should be at least 800 words in order to obtain basic SEO effects.
Key tips to improve readability:
① Control sentence length: Keep each sentence between 15 and 25 words, and avoid overly long and complex sentences.
② Proper segmentation: 3 to 5 sentences per paragraph to give the content more room to breathe.
③ Use subtitles: Use an H2 title every 300 to 500 words to help readers scan quickly.
④ Add to list with bold: Use bulleted lists and bold text to emphasize key points and improve readability.
⑤ Use concise language: Avoid too many technical terms and provide explanations when necessary.
⑥ Add images and multimedia: Visual elements can break the monotony of text and enhance the reading experience.
Keyword Stuffing is the unnatural practice of excessively reusing target keywords in content in an attempt to manipulate search rankings. Search engines such as Google consider keyword stuffing a black hat SEO practice that can result in lower rankings or even removal from search results. Ways to avoid Keyword Stuffing:
① Use synonyms and related words: Naturally change words instead of repeating the same keyword.
② Focus on topic coverage: Make sure your content is comprehensive and valuable, rather than writing for keywords.
③ Keep keyword density below 2.5%: Above this threshold, you need to check whether it is overused.
Heading Structure is an important element of SEO content optimization. The H1 heading tells search engines the core topic of the page, and there should be only one H1 per page. H2 headings are used to organize main sections, and H3 are used for subsections. A good title structure not only helps search engines understand the content hierarchy, but also improves user experience, making it easier for readers to scan and find the information they need. Ideally, content should have 1 H1 and multiple H2s (depending on the length of the content, generally at least one H2 every 300-500 words), with H3 subdivisions where necessary.
A link strategy is an integral part of SEO content marketing.internal link Help search engines understand the structure of the website, transfer weight to important pages, and extend the time users stay on the site. It is recommended that each article have at least 2 to 3 relevant internal links.external link(Linking to other authoritative websites) can increase the credibility and reference value of the content, and search engines will also trust your content more. Properly citing authoritative sources is not only good for SEO, but also provides readers with more valuable resources. Ideally, each article should contain 1 to 3 external links.

SEO content knowledge base

SEO basics

What is Keyword Density?

Keyword density (Keyword Density) refers to the frequency of the target keyword appearing in the article. It is calculated as the number of keyword occurrences × keyword length ÷ total word count × 100%. The ideal density is 0.5% to 2.5%. Too high may be considered keyword stuffing, and too low may not effectively convey the theme of the page. It’s recommended to incorporate keywords naturally into your H1, H2, and first paragraph.

content strategy

What is the ideal article length?

According to SEO research, the average length of the top ten search results is about 1500 to 2500 words. But the "ideal length" depends on the topic and search intent. The focus is on providing complete and valuable information, not on word count. It is generally recommended that the article be at least 800 words and cover the main aspects of the topic. For in-depth content, 2,000 words or more can more comprehensively meet user needs and make it easier to obtain external links.

Readability

How to improve content readability?

Six tips to improve readability: control sentence length (preferably 15 to 25 words), appropriate paragraphing (3 to 5 sentences per paragraph), use subheadings to organize the structure (an H2 every 300 to 500 words), add lists and bold to emphasize key points, use concise language to avoid too many terms, and add pictures and multimedia elements. Good readability not only improves user experience, but also reduces bounce rates and improves search rankings.

SEO Tips

How to avoid Keyword Stuffing?

The best way to avoid keyword stuffing is to get back to the essence of your content: write it for users, not search engines. Use natural substitutions with synonyms and related words, focusing on comprehensive coverage of the topic rather than the repetition of keywords. After writing, check that the keyword density is over 2.5%, and make sure the keywords appear in the title, H1, and first paragraph in a way that sounds natural and smooth.

Content optimization

The impact of Heading structure on SEO

A good Heading structure (H1 → H2 → H3) can help search engines understand the level and focus of the content. The H1 should contain the main keyword and accurately describe the topic of the page (only one per page). H2s are used to organize major chapters, ideally use at least one H2 every 300-500 words. H3 is used to subdivide the subtopics under H2. A clear title hierarchy can significantly improve the SEO performance and user experience of the content.

Advanced knowledge

Content quality vs content length

The core principle of SEO content marketing is “quality over quantity.” The reason why long content ranks better is not because it has more words, but because longer content usually provides more complete information. A 500-word gem article that fully meets user needs may be more effective than a 2,000-word filler article. The focus is on comprehensively answering user questions, covering all relevant aspects, and providing unique value insights.
