Formula and variable relationship

Uses a tropospheric standard-atmosphere approximation from altitude. The implemented expression is “1013.25*Math.pow(1-2.25577e-5*altitude,5.25588)”. The page validates altitude before formatting the result in hPa.

Sensitivity check: change only altitude from 1000 m to 1,100 m. With every other input unchanged, standard pressure moves from 898.75 to 887.9 hPa. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
