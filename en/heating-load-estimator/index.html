Formula and variable relationship

Uses floor area, baseline load density, and climate factor for a first estimate. Heating demand is estimated from floor area, a baseline watts-per-square-metre value, and a climate multiplier. It is an early sizing comparison, not a full envelope, infiltration, and design-temperature heat-loss model. The implemented expression is “area*wattsPerArea*climate”. The page validates floor area, baseline load, climate factor before formatting the result in W.

Sensitivity check: change only floor area from 40 m² to 44 m². With every other input unchanged, estimated heating load moves from 3,220 to 3,542 W. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
