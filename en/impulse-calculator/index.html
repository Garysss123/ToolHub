Formula and variable relationship

Multiplies constant force by duration to estimate impulse. The implemented expression is “force*time”. The page validates average force, duration before formatting the result in N·s.

Sensitivity check: change only average force from 500 N to 550 N. With every other input unchanged, impulse moves from 100 to 110 N·s. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
