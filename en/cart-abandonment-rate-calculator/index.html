Formula and variable relationship

Calculates the share of initiated carts that did not complete. The implemented expression is “(started-completed)/started*100”. The page validates checkouts started, completed orders before formatting the result in %.

Sensitivity check: change only checkouts started from 5200 to 5,720 . With every other input unchanged, cart abandonment rate moves from 34.62 to 40.56 %. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
