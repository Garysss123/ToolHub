Formula and variable relationship

Uses τ = L/R to estimate current response in an RL circuit. An RL time constant describes inductor-current rise or decay and follows L/R. The winding resistance of a real coil must be included in the total circuit resistance. The implemented expression is “(l/1000)/r*1000”. The page validates inductance, resistance before formatting the result in ms.

Sensitivity check: change only inductance from 100 mH to 110 mH. With every other input unchanged, time constant moves from 2 to 2.2 ms. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
