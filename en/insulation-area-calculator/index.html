Formula and variable relationship

Subtracts openings and adds cutting allowance for insulation purchase area. The implemented expression is “Math.max(0,gross-openings)*(1+waste/100)”. The page validates gross wall area, openings, waste before formatting the result in m².

Sensitivity check: change only gross wall area from 100 m² to 110 m². With every other input unchanged, purchase area moves from 87.74 to 98.44 m². This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
