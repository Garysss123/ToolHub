QR Code/Barcode Reader Complete User Guide

Code Reader recognizes QR Codes and 1D barcodes from camera screens or pictures. The identification result is only the data content and does not mean that the URL, payment information or downloaded file is safe; you should check the domain and complete string before opening the link.

Operation steps

  1. Allow camera or select a clear, complete barcode image
  2. Keep the code surface flat, well-lit and avoid cutting blank spaces
  3. Read the decoded text before deciding whether to copy or open the link

Practical suggestions and limitations

Blur, reflection, low contrast and excessive perspective will reduce the recognition rate. Unfamiliar QR Codes may lead to phishing websites, please do not automatically trust the scan results.

Privacy and native processing

The image and decoding results are processed locally in the browser, and the camera footage will not be uploaded.
