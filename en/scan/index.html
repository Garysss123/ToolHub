Complete guide to using Photo to Scan file scanning tool

Photo to Scan organizes document photos into an easier-to-read scanning style, suitable for receipts, notes, and general paper backups. Perspective correction, cropping, and contrast enhancement can improve readability, but they cannot restore details that were not captured or were overexposed in the original photo.

Operation steps

  1. Choose clear, complete document photos without serious reflections
  2. Adjust the four corners and cropping range, and then choose color, grayscale or high-contrast effects
  3. Zoom in to check the text and seal, and then output the file after confirmation.

Practical suggestions and limitations

When shooting, keep the lens as parallel to the paper as possible, use uniform light and increase the original resolution. Important contracts or certificates should still be kept in original form, and confirm whether the receiving unit accepts digital scanning.

Privacy and native processing

Photos and file contents are only used in the local image processing process and will not be uploaded.
