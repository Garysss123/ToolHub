Formula and variable relationship

Estimates inverter VA from load, power factor, and safety headroom. The implemented expression is “load/pf*(1+safety/100)”. The page validates continuous load, power factor, safety margin before formatting the result in VA.

Sensitivity check: change only continuous load from 1200 W to 1,320 W. With every other input unchanged, recommended capacity moves from 1,765 to 1,941 VA. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
