Formula and variable relationship

Estimates effective words typed from speed, time, and accuracy. The implemented expression is “speed*minutes*(accuracy/100)”. The page validates typing speed, typing time, accuracy before formatting the result in word.

Sensitivity check: change only typing speed from 55 WPM to 61 WPM. With every other input unchanged, effective words typed moves from 2,376 to 2,614 word. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
