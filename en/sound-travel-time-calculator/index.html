Formula and variable relationship

Adjusts sound speed for air temperature before calculating travel time. The implemented expression is “distance/(331.3+0.606*temp)”. The page validates distance, air temperature before formatting the result in s.

Sensitivity check: change only distance from 1000 m to 1,100 m. With every other input unchanged, travel time moves from 2.912 to 3.203 s. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
