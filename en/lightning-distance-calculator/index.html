Reproduce the built-in example

After validating flash-to-thunder delay, air temperature, the page displays its result in the output workspace and enables the relevant copy or download action.

Formula and variable relationship

Uses flash-to-thunder delay and temperature-adjusted sound speed. The implemented expression is “seconds*(331.3+0.606*temp)/1000”. The page validates flash-to-thunder delay, air temperature before formatting the result in km.

Sensitivity check: change only flash-to-thunder delay from 9 s to 9.9 s. With every other input unchanged, estimated distance moves from 3.09 to 3.4 km. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
