QR Code Generator Complete User Guide

The QR Code Generator can encode URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi or contact information into QR codes that can be scanned by your phone's camera. The content should be confirmed to be correct before generation, and sufficient tolerance should be reserved for the actual printing size, light and scanning distance.

Operation steps

  1. Select the profile type and enter the full content you want to share
  2. Adjust size, color and tolerance levels to maintain high foreground and background contrast
  3. After downloading, scan it with different mobile phones to confirm that the content and links are safe.

Practical suggestions and limitations

The white space around the QR Code cannot be cropped; error tolerance should be improved when adding a logo or printing on a complex background, but too large a decorative area will still cause it to be unrecognizable.

Privacy and native processing

Input content and image code rendering are completed on the current page, and the tool will not send the URL, Wi-Fi password or contact information.
