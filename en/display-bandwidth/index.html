Display Interface Bandwidth Calculator Complete User Guide

Display bandwidth depends on horizontal and vertical pixels, update rate, bits per pixel, and the encoding and blanking overhead of the transport protocol. The calculation results can be used to initially determine whether a specific display mode is close to the upper limit of the interface.

Operation steps

  1. Enter resolution, refresh rate, color depth, and color sampling settings
  2. Compare raw pixel data rates to requirements including overhead
  3. Compare the specifications supported by graphics cards, screens, cables and ports

Practical suggestions and limitations

The nominal interface bandwidth does not mean that the entire bandwidth is available for image data. DSC, chroma sampling and timing formats will change the feasible model, and ultimately the result of the complete device chain negotiation will prevail.

Privacy and native processing

All parameters and deductions are done in the browser, no hardware data is detected or transmitted.
