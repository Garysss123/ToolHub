Video Bitrate Calculator Complete User Guide

Video file size is mainly determined by the total bit rate and playback time. The calculator can infer the available image bitrate from the target capacity, and can also estimate the finished product size from the given bitrate, which is suitable for transcoding, recording and storage planning.

Operation steps

  1. Enter video length, audio bitrate and target size or image bitrate
  2. Comparing decimal/binary capacity to container overhead
  3. Actual coding with short videos to check motion scenes, details and platform limitations

Practical suggestions and limitations

The same bit rate will have different image quality in different encoders, Codecs, resolutions and contents. The instantaneous peak value of VBR may also be higher than the average value, so you need to reserve upload bandwidth when streaming.

Privacy and native processing

Video parameters are only calculated locally, and the tool will not read or upload videos.
