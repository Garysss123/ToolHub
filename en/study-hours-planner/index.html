Formula and variable relationship

Spreads topic study hours evenly across remaining days. The implemented expression is “topics*hoursPerTopic/days”. The page validates topics remaining, hours per topic, days remaining before formatting the result in h.

Sensitivity check: change only topics remaining from 18 to 19.8 . With every other input unchanged, suggested study per day moves from 2.14 to 2.36 h. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
