Complete User Guide to IP Footprint and Network Information Inspection

The IP and Network Footprint tool displays connection information observable by browsers and network services, helping to understand the differences between public IP, local network, proxy or privacy protection. Browsers are subject to permissions and privacy policies, and the results are not equivalent to a complete security scan.

Operation steps

  1. Open the tool in the expected network environment and record the displayed items
  2. Compare differences after switching VPNs, proxies or different connections
  3. If an abnormality is found, further confirm it from the router, DNS and trusted security tools.

Practical suggestions and limitations

IP addresses do not reliably identify an individual or precise location, nor do they alone prove a breach. Please do not conduct unauthorized probes on networks that do not belong to you.

Privacy and native processing

The page presents only the information required for the tool to function; do not share the results publicly as they may contain data that can be used for network identification.
