Frequently asked questions about aspect ratio and size conversion

What is Aspect Ratio?

Aspect Ratio It is the proportional relationship between the width and height of the image, usually expressed as an integer ratio of "width:height", for example 16:9 Represents the width as 1.78 times the height.

For example, a 1920 × 1080 For the picture, after dividing the width and height by the greatest common factor 120, we get 16 : 9。

The aspect ratio determines the image'sshow shape：

  • 16:9 — Widescreen, ideal for video and TV
  • 4:3 — Close to square, traditional screen and old photos
  • 1:1 — Perfect circle, often seen in social posts
  • 9:16 — Upright style, suitable for mobile phone portrait videos
What are the 16:9 screen resolutions?

16:9 It is currently the most mainstream display ratio. Common resolutions include:

  • 640 × 360 — nHD
  • 1280 × 720 — HD（720p）
  • 1920 × 1080 — Full HD（1080p）
  • 2560 × 1440 — 2K / QHD（1440p）
  • 3840 × 2160 — 4K / UHD
  • 7680 × 4320 — 8K

Each level of resolution is twice the previous one, but the ratio is maintained at 16:9, so the same image can be enlarged without loss.

💡 Checking method: Enter any resolution into this tool, if it displays 16:9 That is the standard wide screen ratio.

What is YouTube's recommended video to thumbnail ratio?

YouTube provides support for multiple formats. The main recommendations are as follows:

Video (general): 16:9 landscape - 1920×1080 or 3840×2160 recommended

Shorts (short videos):9:16 Upright — 1080×1920 recommended

Thumbnail:16:9 — 1280×720 recommended, less than 2MB

Key points of thumbnail design:

  • Thumbnails are the most important factor in determining click-through rates and account for the largest visual area of search results.
  • Important text and image elements shouldstay in central safe zone, to avoid being obscured by time stamps and titles
  • Use high-contrast colors and large fonts to make them legible even on small screens

Instagram supports multiple post formats, each with recommended sizes:

  • General Post (Post):1:1 square — 1080×1080
  • Portrait：4:5 — 1080×1350
  • Landscape：16:9 — 1080×608
  • Limited time news (Story)：9:16 — 1080×1920
  • Reels:9:16 Upright — 1080×1920

💡 It is recommended to use the highest resolution version (such as 1080×1080) when uploading, Instagram will automatically compress it; uploading with a low resolution will enlarge and cause blur.

  • The video length is recommended to be 21-34 seconds, and grab the audience’s attention in the first 2 seconds
  • Safe area design: avoid placing important content at the bottom of the screen (it may be blocked by the interface)

Why are some ratios not whole numbers (e.g. 205:78)?

Not all dimensions can be reduced to "memorable" whole-number ratios. For example, the recommended dimensions for Facebook cover images are 820 × 312, its greatest common factor is 4, and after reduction it is 205 : 78——This is its true proportion.

💡 The decimal ratio is more valuable for reference:

820 ÷ 312 ≈ 2.63 — means the width is approximately 2.63 times the height

A4（210×297）≈ 0.71 — represents a width approximately 0.71 times the height (straight)

This tool displays both the reduced integer ratio and the decimal ratio, allowing you to accurately understand the actual proportion of any size.

How to calculate aspect ratio?

Aspect ratio is calculated by finding the ratio of width to heightGreatest common divisor (GCD), and then divide both sides by that number:

Example: 1920 × 1080

GCD(1920, 1080) = 120

1920 ÷ 120 = 16，1080 ÷ 120 = 9

Result:16 : 9

If the two numbers have no common factors (GCD = 1), further simplification is not possible, for example 1024 × 768 The GCD is 256, which can be simplified to 4:3; while some sizes (such as Facebook cover 820×312) are still larger integers (205:78) after reduction, and the decimal ratio (2.63) is a more intuitive reference.
