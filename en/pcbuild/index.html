Complete Guide to Using Computer Parts Price Calculator

Use the Computer Parts Price Calculator to summarize the expenses of building your own PC and quickly see the total price and major cost sources. It doesn't replace compatibility checking, but it can help compare different configurations, operating systems and peripheral costs.

Operation steps

  1. Fill in the part name, quantity and actual tax-included price according to category
  2. Check the total price and the proportion of each category, and establish a comparison of alternative configurations
  3. Complete items that are easily missed such as cooling, power supply, case fan, and shipping costs.

Practical suggestions and limitations

The lowest price does not necessarily mean the highest overall value. When planning, consider slots, size, power supply, cooling, warranty, and room for future upgrades, and reserve a budget for price fluctuations.

Privacy and native processing

All equipment names and prices are only calculated in the browser, and no cloud list will be created or shopping information will be sent.
