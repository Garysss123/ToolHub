Formula and variable relationship

Uses t = √(2h/g) from rest while ignoring air resistance. The implemented expression is “Math.sqrt(2*height/g)”. The page validates drop height, gravity before formatting the result in s.

Sensitivity check: change only drop height from 100 m to 110 m. With every other input unchanged, fall time moves from 4.516 to 4.736 s. This provides a reproducible input-to-result check.
